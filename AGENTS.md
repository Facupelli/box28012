# BOX 28012

BOX 28012 is a functional training center in Lavapiés, Madrid. It is a neighborhood box built around small-group and personal training, with an emphasis on close professional attention, good technique, progression, and community.

The website is a marketing site whose main objective is to turn visitors into real contacts, primarily through WhatsApp and the **“¡PRUEBA GRATIS!”** CTA. It should communicate the character of the box clearly and make the experience feel approachable, professional, and local rather than like a generic fitness brand.

BOX 28012 is strongly connected to **Lavapiés and the 28012 postal code**. That local identity is an important part of the brand. The site should feel rooted in its neighborhood without becoming Madrid-themed or tourist-oriented.

The visual direction is that of a **contemporary Madrid neighborhood institution**: editorial rather than sporty, physical rather than digital, and disciplined rather than aggressive.

The overall feeling should be **local, recognizable, warm, strong, architectural, tactile, contemporary, and restrained**.

Photography is a major part of the identity. Favor real, warm, dimensional images of the box, its materials, its people, and training in progress. The design should frame the photography rather than overpower it.

Typography and composition should feel closer to **editorial design, signage, and physical graphic design** than to product UI. Use strong alignment, generous space, deliberate image crops, rectangular geometry, and clear typographic hierarchy.

The brand palette is built around near-black and charcoal surfaces, warm off-white, and a restrained BOX red accent. Red should act as punctuation or emphasis, not decoration.

Avoid generic premium-gym aesthetics, aggressive performance branding, SaaS-like components, rounded cards, glassmorphism, gradients, neon effects, excessive decoration, dense collages, fake street-art styling, and visual elements that exist only to fill empty space.

A useful principle for all design decisions is: **fewer, stronger gestures**. Every visual element should communicate content, create hierarchy, establish identity, or structure information. If it does none of those things, it probably does not belong.
